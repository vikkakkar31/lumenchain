# Participation Design

> Status: Draft

## Goal

Define how network participants contribute useful, measurable activity and how that activity is accounted for without compromising chain security or economic sustainability.

## Participation Lifecycle

1. Registration or eligibility check
2. Participation activity
3. Verification and accounting
4. Reward calculation
5. Distribution and auditability

## Design Requirements

- Rules must be deterministic.
- Reward eligibility must be independently verifiable.
- Anti-abuse mechanisms must be explicit.
- Participation accounting must not permit double claiming.
- Parameter changes must be versioned and governed.

## Open Questions

- Participant types and roles
- Eligibility requirements
- Measurement methodology
- Reward frequency
- Penalties and dispute handling
